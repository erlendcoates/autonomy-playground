LOS Guidance

Line-of-sight (LOS) guidance on a waypoint path, with two ways of choosing the reference point on the path: the orthogonal projection of the vessel, and a propagated path particle. Vessels are modelled as point particles with a first-order velocity lag and drawn pointing along their velocity. The path is straight legs joined by polynomial transition curves (Kobryń, 2014), closed into a loop by default; the dashed line joins the waypoints. Drag the square handles to reshape the path while the vessels are moving, drag a vessel to move it.

Guidance: lookahead-based line of sight in vector form, with the reference point either projected onto the path or propagated with a path-variable update law. Path: straight legs joined by polynomial transition curves (Kobryń, 2014). Video: Line-of-Sight (LOS) Guidance Explained; more in the playlist Guidance and Control.
