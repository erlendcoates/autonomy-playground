Autonomy Playground

Interactive apps on guidance, navigation and control of autonomous vehicles. Each app lets you drag, change a gain, run it again, and watch what happens. Everything runs in your browser.

On YouTube: short narrated lessons with simulations of surface vessels, underwater vehicles and drones.

▶ The YouTube channel

Guidance

LOS Guidance

Line-of-sight (LOS) guidance for following a waypoint-based path with smooth turns. Drag the waypoints while two vessels follow the path, and compare two ways of choosing the reference point on the path.

Open the app → ▶ Watch the video

State estimation

State Observer

A ball and beam with a noisy distance sensor. Estimate the ball's velocity by finite differences, a filtered derivative or a Luenberger observer, choose which one the controller uses, and tune the observer's gains.
